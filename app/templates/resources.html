Yamadori baseline checklist

Use this as your default in-field checklist for tree finds. Run AI only for find-specific interpretation.

Tree quality

  • Trunk movement, taper, and base thickness relative to target design.
  • Nebari potential: visible root flare, radial spread, and bury depth.
  • Bark character and signs of age that fit your intended style.
  • Primary branch options and back-budding potential near key cut points.

Health and recovery odds

  • Vigor: bud density, healthy foliage color, no major dieback.
  • Stress signals: pests, fungal issues, trunk wounds, severe lean damage.
  • Root zone conditions: compaction, rock, drainage, moisture pattern.
  • Aftercare feasibility at home: shade, watering frequency, wintering plan.

Extraction practicality

  • Access and carrying route from tree to vehicle.
  • Estimated rootball size and tools required on-site.
  • Safety risks: slope stability, traffic, weather, trip hazards.

Legal and ethical checks

  • Landowner permission is explicit and documented.
  • Species/site is not protected or restricted for collection.
  • Collection impact is minimal and site is left stable.

Decision shortcut

Collect now only if quality, health, legality, and aftercare are all favorable. Otherwise keep as a watch find and revisit in a better season.
